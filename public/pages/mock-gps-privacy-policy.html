1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mock GPS (“the app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Android application.

The app is designed to let users simulate GPS locations and, where available, create or play simulated routes such as circular routes or routes drawn manually. Use of mock-location features may affect how other apps interpret your device's location.

2. Information and permissions

Depending on the features you use and the Android version on your device, the app may request or process the following:

  • Location information: coordinates used to set or simulate a location or to run a route.
  • Route and preference data: route points, speed, route shape, repeat/overlap settings, and app preferences that you enter or save.
  • Device and diagnostic information: information made available by Android or by third-party SDKs, if those services are integrated into the version you use.

We do not ask you to provide your name, phone number, or account credentials to use the app unless a future feature explicitly requires them.

3. Location and mock routes

Location-related permissions and Android's mock-location capability are used to provide the app's core functionality. You may need to enable the relevant mock-location setting in Android Developer Options.

  • Coordinates and route points you choose may be used to calculate and simulate the route.
  • The simulated location may be exposed by Android to other apps on your device that request location access.
  • Mock location is not a guarantee that every app or service will accept or use the simulated coordinates.

Do not use the app to mislead others, violate a service's terms, or bypass laws or safety requirements. You are responsible for how you use simulated location data.

4. How information is used

Information processed by the app may be used to:

  • provide location simulation and route playback;
  • remember settings or routes, if saving is supported;
  • troubleshoot errors and improve reliability, if diagnostics are enabled; and
  • respond to messages you send to our support contact.

6. Storage and retention

Where routes and preferences are stored locally, they remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android's storage behavior and any backups you have enabled.

If a future version adds cloud backup, account login, or synchronization, this policy will need to explain what is uploaded, where it is stored, how long it is retained, and how you can delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the app's design to protect information under our control. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the matter.

Where local law sets a different age or additional requirements, those requirements apply.

9. Your choices

  • You can manage app permissions in Android Settings.
  • You can stop using mock-location features or disable the relevant Developer Options setting.
  • You can remove locally saved app data through Android Settings or uninstall the app.
  • You can contact us with privacy questions or requests. We will handle requests as required by applicable law.

10. Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes or to reflect legal, technical, or operational requirements. We will update the “Last updated” date above when changes are made. Continued use after an updated policy is published is subject to the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.
