Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the Deeplink Tester Android application.

Effective date: October 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Deeplink Tester handles information when you use the Deeplink Tester Android application ("App").

Deeplink Tester is a utility application designed to help developers and users enter, test, and verify Android deep links and app links.

2. Information We Collect

Deeplink Tester does not collect or sell personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, payment information, or passwords.

3. Deep Links and URLs

Deeplink Tester allows you to enter URLs, deep links, and app links for testing.

The deep links and URLs entered into the App are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by Deeplink Tester.

You should avoid entering passwords, authentication tokens, personal information, or other confidential information into a URL or deep link while testing.

4. Device Information

Deeplink Tester does not intentionally collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, precise location information, contacts, or other personal device information.

The App only uses the Android functionality necessary to perform its core deep-link testing features.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Deeplink Tester does not send the deep links you enter to external services for advertising or analytics purposes.

6. Analytics and Advertising

Deeplink Tester does not use third-party advertising services or analytics services to track your activity within the App.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Deeplink Tester does not maintain a server-side account or database containing your personal information.

Any temporary information required for testing is handled locally on your device. Because we do not collect personal information on our servers, there is no server-side personal data retained by us.

8. Third-Party Applications

When you test a deep link, Deeplink Tester may request Android to open another application installed on your device.

Once another application is opened, that application's handling of information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms. Deeplink Tester does not control the privacy practices of third-party applications.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection or transmission of information through the App. However, no software or electronic transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Deeplink Tester is a general-purpose developer utility and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality or data practices of Deeplink Tester change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Deeplink Tester, please contact us.
